How it works

A clear estimate, step by step.

Fare Keralam uses your selected vehicle details and distance to request a calculation from its fare service.

  1. 01

    Select the vehicle details

    Choose a category and provide seating or model details when asked.

  2. 02

    Enter the distance

    Use the total journey distance in kilometres.

  3. 03

    Review the estimate

    See the fare, applicable rule details and any distance-slab breakdown returned by the service.

Please note

The amount is an estimate based on fare data currently available to the service. It does not replace a current government order or the operator’s fare information.
